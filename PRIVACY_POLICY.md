# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated:** July 28, 2026  
**Application:** Scientific Sun Exposure Timer  
**Package Name:** `com.suntimer.app`  

At **Scientific Sun Exposure Timer**, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines transparently how data, device permissions, and sensitive information are handled within the app.

Our project strictly applies the principle of privacy by design and by default. **We do not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal or sensitive user data.**

---

## 1. Data Processing and Device Permissions

To calculate the recommended sun exposure time accurately, the app requires real-time access to specific device features and sensors:

### a) Location Data (GPS)
* **Usage:** The app requests access to your GPS location only if permission is explicitly granted by the user. This data is used strictly in real-time to query the current UV Index for your area via an open weather service (Open-Meteo API).
* **Storage:** **None.** Your geographic location is not stored in any database, logged on the device, or transmitted to any proprietary or third-party server for tracking or advertising purposes.

### b) Skin Phototype (Health/Physical Data)
* **Usage:** During setup, the app allows you to select your skin phototype (Fitzpatrick Scale) to determine your skin's estimated resistance to solar radiation.
* **Storage:** This information is processed locally on your device to perform time calculations. It is never stored on external servers nor linked to any personal identity or user profile.

### c) Ambient Light Sensor (Lux)
* **Usage:** The app reads the phone's ambient light sensor value to determine whether the device is under direct sunlight or shade, dynamically adjusting the estimated radiation received.
* **Storage:** Lux readings are processed in real time on your device and are neither stored nor transmitted anywhere.

---

## 2. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. The app does not integrate advertising networks, tracking analytics SDKs, or user profiling tools.

The only network request the app performs is an anonymous technical call to a public weather API (Open-Meteo) sending latitude and longitude coordinates to retrieve the current local UV index.

---

## 3. Advertising & Monetization

The app contains **NO advertisements** of any kind (no banners, no native ads, no third-party ad networks).

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Scientific Sun Exposure Timer is a general-audience application focused on health and sun protection. Because we do not collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from any user, the application is safe for all audiences, including children.

---

## 5. User Rights (GDPR)

Since we do not store personal data on external servers or identify individual users, we hold no personal records to modify or delete. You can revoke location permissions or clear local app data at any time through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.
